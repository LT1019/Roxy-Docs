# Roxy Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 19 October 2026

Roxy is a Discord bot that tracks activity to provide levels, profiles, leaderboards and achievements. This policy explains what Roxy stores, why, and how you can have it deleted.

## What Roxy stores

| Data | Why |
|---|---|
| Your Discord user ID, username and display name | To identify your profile and show your name on leaderboards |
| The **number** of messages you send, in total and per server | To award XP, run server levels and rank the message leaderboard |
| Your XP and level in each server | To show your server profile and give rank roles that server's admins set up |
| Games shown in your Discord status, with start time, end time and duration | To show gaming statistics and award XP |
| Apps shown in your Discord status that aren't games (for example Visual Studio Code, YouTube or Netflix), with start time, end time and duration | To show app statistics and award XP |
| Songs shown in your Discord status by Spotify, YouTube Music or another music app (title, artist, album and which app), with start time, end time and duration | To show listening statistics and award XP |
| Time spent in voice channels (server, channel name, start time, end time and duration) | To show voice statistics and award XP |
| Your level, XP and achievements, including achievements a server's admins give you in that server | To run the levelling system and show achievements on your profile |
| The date Roxy first and last saw you | To show your timeline and remove inactive records |

## What Roxy does not store

- **The content of your messages.** Roxy counts messages; she never saves what they say.
- Voice chat, direct messages between users, or anything from servers Roxy is not in.
- Passwords, email addresses, payment details or your IP address.

## How the data is used

Your data is used **only** to provide Roxy's features: profiles, leaderboards, statistics and achievements.

Roxy's developer does not:

- sell your data,
- share it with third parties,
- use it for advertising, or
- use it for anything other than running Roxy.

## Who can see your data

- **Other Discord users** can see your Roxy profile, statistics and leaderboard position in servers you share with Roxy. The global leaderboard shows your display name and score to users in other servers.
- **Roxy's developer** can access the database to run and maintain the bot.

## Patreon supporters

Payments are handled entirely by Patreon. Roxy never receives your payment details. She only checks whether you have a supporter role in the Roxy Discord server.

## Seeing or deleting your data

You can ask Roxy's developer to:

- **tell you what is stored** about you, or
- **permanently delete** everything stored about you.

To make a request, join the Roxy support server through the **Support** button under `rr help` and ask there. Requests are handled as soon as possible, and within 30 days at most.

Deletion cannot be undone. If you keep using Discord in servers where Roxy is present, she starts tracking your activity again from zero.

## How long data is kept

Your data is kept until you ask for it to be deleted. Records for users with no activity at all may be removed during maintenance.

## Security

The database is stored on the server that runs Roxy and is not publicly accessible. Access is limited to Roxy's developer.

## Children

Discord requires users to be at least 13 years old (or older in some countries). Roxy is not intended for anyone below Discord's minimum age.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the effective date above is updated and the change is announced in the Roxy support server.

## Contact

Questions or requests: join the Roxy support server through the **Support** button under `rr help`.
